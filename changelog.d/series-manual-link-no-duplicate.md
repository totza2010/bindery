### Fixed
- **Adding a book no longer creates a second copy of a series you linked by hand** — creating a series yourself, linking it to Hardcover, then adding a book from that series left the library holding two series with the same name: the one you linked, and a twin created automatically alongside it. A hand-made series keeps the identifier it was created with and records the Hardcover one separately, as the link; the code that files an incoming book under its series only ever looked at the first, found nothing, and made a new series. It now checks the link too, so a series you have already told Bindery about is the one the book joins. Nothing is rewritten in the process — the series keeps its own identifier and its manual label. Series linked to other providers, and series nothing has been linked to, behave exactly as before; where nothing says two same-named series are the same, Bindery still declines to guess. Duplicates already created are not merged, and can be deleted from the series page.
