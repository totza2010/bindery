### Added
- **Search results in Add Book now say which catalogue each came from** — a search fans out across every configured provider and returns them merged, so one title arrives several times over: searching *Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix* returned seven rows, among them the novel, the sheet music credited to John Williams, the film score credited to Nicholas Hooper, a Ladybird children's edition, and two entries carrying no author at all. They were indistinguishable without adding one and looking at what turned up. Each result now carries the name of the provider that supplied it, so the Hardcover record and the OpenLibrary one can be told apart before choosing. Results whose provider is unrecorded — older entries predating the field — fall back to the prefix on their foreign ID, and show no label when even that says nothing.
