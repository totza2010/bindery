### Fixed
- **The missing books past the eighth in a series can now be reached** — a series card listed eight and then said how many more there were, which was true and useless: there was no way to see them, no scrolling and nothing to click. A series with 34 entries showed eight of them. That line is now a control that reveals the rest, scrolling within the card so one long series cannot push everything else off the page, and folds them away again.

### Changed
- **Add Book scrolls as one list rather than a box inside a box** — the results had their own capped, scrolling area inside the dialog's own scrolling body, so the page had two scrollbars and the wheel moved whichever one the pointer happened to be over. The dialog now scrolls as a single region with its heading and buttons fixed in place.
