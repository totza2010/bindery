### Added
- **A Hardcover book or author now links out to its page on hardcover.app**, the way an OpenLibrary or Google Books record already did. Hardcover was on the list of providers whose stored IDs were held not to map to a stable public page, but that had stopped being true: Bindery stores `hc:<slug>` and hardcover.app routes both `/books/<slug>` and `/authors/<slug>` on exactly that slug. The link appears wherever the metadata source is already shown — the book and author detail pages, and the provider rows on a book. Records that reached Bindery without a slug keep no link: the importer falls back to the numeric id for those, and hardcover.app returns 404 for it, so a missing link is preferred to a broken one.
